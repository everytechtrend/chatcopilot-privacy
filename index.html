ChatCopilot AI — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

1. Overview

ChatCopilot AI ("the extension") is a browser extension that reads the text content of the web page you are viewing and uses an AI provider — configured by you — to generate suggested chat replies. This policy explains exactly what data is handled and where it goes.

2. What data the extension processes

  • Page text content: When you click "Suggest Replies", the text content of the currently active tab is extracted and sent to the AI provider you selected in Settings. This is done only on your explicit action.
  • Your instructions and settings: Your major prompt, instructions, addons, tone, language and typing preferences are stored locally in your browser.
  • API keys: The API keys you provide (Gemini and/or OpenRouter) are stored locally in your browser using Chrome's storage and are sent only to the corresponding provider when generating replies.
  • License key (Pro users): If you purchase a Pro subscription, your license information is stored locally and validated against the Freemius licensing API.

3. What we do NOT do

  • We do not collect, store, or transmit your page content, API keys, or generated replies to the developer or any developer-operated server.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties for advertising or tracking.
  • We do not read pages in the background — reading happens only when you click a button.
  • We do not access your browsing history, bookmarks, or data from other websites.

4. Third-party services

Data flows directly from your browser to these services, governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google Gemini API (if you configure it): page text is sent to generate replies — Google Privacy Policy
  • OpenRouter (if you configure it): page text is sent to the model provider you select — OpenRouter Privacy Policy
  • Freemius (Pro subscribers only): processes your payment and license — Freemius Privacy Policy

AI providers may retain prompt data according to their own policies; review them if this matters to you. Do not paste sensitive personal data into chats you process with the extension.

5. Data storage

All extension settings, keys, and license data are stored using Chrome's local/synced storage on your own browser profile. Uninstalling the extension removes this data.

6. Your choices

  • You choose which AI provider receives page text (Settings).
  • You can remove your license (Settings → License → Remove license).
  • You can cancel your subscription anytime via the payment page or by contacting us.
